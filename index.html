FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Email ehsanrazzaq55@gmail.com.

Is SaveBox free?

Yes. The free plan gives everyone 10 downloads a day, 2 at a time, at up to the best quality the source offers — with or without an account.

Do I need an account?

No. A fresh install works immediately as a guest, with its own history. If you create an account later, it upgrades that guest in place, so nothing you've already downloaded is lost.

Where do my videos go?

Into Movies/SaveBox on your phone, where they show up in your gallery app like anything else you've recorded. They belong to you — deleting the app or its data does not touch files you've already saved.

Which sites does it work with?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, including their short links.

What quality and format do I get?

You choose best available, 720p or 480p before the download starts. Every file is an H.264/AAC MP4 with fast-start, so it plays natively on any phone.

Do you keep copies of my videos?

Only long enough for your phone to fetch them. The server copy is deleted automatically within 24 hours, and once a file is on your phone we have no access to it.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account in the app and confirm, or email us from your account's address. It's a hard delete — see delete your account for exactly what is removed.

Can I download any video?

SaveBox is a tool. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to download and keep any video you save with it, and for respecting the terms of the site it came from and the rights of whoever made it.
