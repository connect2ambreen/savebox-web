How it works

Three steps, and the last one happens on its own.

1

Paste the link

Copy a video URL from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook and paste it in. SaveBox shows you the title, length and thumbnail first.

2

Pick a quality

Best available, 720p or 480p — whichever suits the video and the space you have. You see the choice before anything downloads.

3

It lands in your gallery

The finished video is saved to Movies/SaveBox and shows up in your gallery app like anything else you've recorded.

Built to just work

The details that decide whether a download is actually usable.

Plays on any device

Every download is converted to an H.264/AAC MP4 with fast-start enabled, so it opens natively on any phone instead of needing a particular player.

No account required

A fresh install works immediately as a guest, with its own history. Creating an account is optional, and upgrades that guest in place — nothing you've already downloaded is lost.

Your history, kept

Every download is recorded, so you can see what you saved and when — even after the file itself has been cleared from the server.

Nothing lingers

Files are held on the server just long enough for your phone to fetch them — 24 hours — then deleted automatically.
